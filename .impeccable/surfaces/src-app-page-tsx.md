# Study Space core application

- **Scope:** Today, History, Leaderboard, Profile, and their shared navigation/design primitives.
- **Mode:** Operate.
- **Audience and job:** Independent learners using iPhone or Mac to begin focused work, understand pace, and feel quiet accountability to a chosen circle.
- **Primary action:** Start a study session from Today; setup may preview goal and length but timer behavior remains out of scope.
- **Content:** Realistic illustrative personal, progress, session, and circle data, always labeled when it could imply live data.
- **Constraints:** Frontend only; no persistence, authentication, Supabase, database, or real timer. Avoid equal-card dashboards, gradient language, badges, gamified ranking, clutter, and childlike motifs.
- **Direction:** In Concert—light ivory field, ink, coral, moss, and sky; fine measure lines; expressive scale; individual progress and social context as parallel voices.
- **Memorable moment:** The first Today measure resolves into one broad coral Start Study Session control, with personal pace and quiet company continuing below rather than competing beside it.
- **Responsive behavior:** Mobile bottom navigation; desktop top navigation. Dense rows recompose structurally instead of shrinking or becoming carousels.
- **Open decisions:** Final production name/identity assets, narrower launch audience, and whether future social activity should show live co-presence, completed sessions, or both.
